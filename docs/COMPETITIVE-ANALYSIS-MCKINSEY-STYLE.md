# OmniGaze Competitive Analysis
## McKinsey-Style Strategic Assessment

**Date:** January 2026
**Competitors Analyzed:** Bullwall (calculator), vScope, Faddom, Device42, ServiceNow ITOM, ValueBlue (BlueDolphin), Ardoq, LeanIX

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis examines 8 competitors across the IT infrastructure discovery and enterprise architecture space to identify gaps in OmniGaze's current website and marketing approach. The findings reveal significant opportunities in interactive tools, trust signals, content marketing, and conversion optimization.

**Key Finding:** OmniGaze's unique "servers to strategy" positioning is differentiated but under-leveraged. Competitors excel at quantifying value, building trust through analyst recognition, and providing self-service evaluation tools.

- Number of applications

## ROI Calculator Improvements

### Current State Assessment

OmniGaze's current ROI calculator has:
- ✅ Good input fields (servers, applications, incidents, hours, rate)
- ✅ Visual empty state with preview
- ❌ Static calculations (no animation)
- ❌ Single time frame
- ❌ No export/share functionality
- ❌ No third-party data citations
- ❌ Limited visual impact in results

### Recommended Enhancements

#### Phase 1: Quick Wins (1 week)

1. **Add Real-Time Cost Counter**
   - Show "Your hidden costs are accumulating..."
   - Animated counter showing costs per second/minute/hour

2. **Add Currency Selector**
   - USD, EUR, GBP, DKK, SEK, NOK

3. **Add Industry Benchmarks**
   - "Companies your size typically see X% improvement"
   - Cite industry research (Gartner, IDC, Forrester)

4. **Multi-Year Projections**
   - Show 1-year, 3-year, 5-year ROI
   - Include OmniGaze licensing cost comparison

#### Phase 2: Enhanced Functionality (2-3 weeks)

5. **Email Results Feature**
   - "Email this report to your team"
   - Capture email for lead generation
   - Professional PDF format

6. **Comparison Mode**
   - "Traditional Tools" vs "OmniGaze" side-by-side
   - Show time/cost for manual approach vs automated

7. **Risk Score Visualization**
   - Traffic light system (Red/Yellow/Green)
   - Categories: Documentation Risk, Compliance Risk, Operational Risk

8. **Animated Charts**
   - Use Framer Motion or Lottie for chart animations
   - Bar chart race showing cost accumulation

#### Phase 3: Advanced Features (1 month)

9. **Industry-Specific Calculations**
   - Different multipliers for Finance, Healthcare, etc.
   - Industry dropdown selection

10. **Integration with Demo Request**
    - "See these savings in action - Book a personalized demo"
    - Pre-fill demo form with calculator inputs

11. **Shareable Results Page**
    - Unique URL for calculated results
    - Social sharing buttons
    - Embeddable for internal presentations

## Conclusion

OmniGaze has a unique market position bridging infrastructure discovery and enterprise architecture. However, the website underutilizes this differentiation compared to competitors who excel at:

1. **Quantifying value** (LeanIX savings calculator, Ardoq's "$5M saved")
2. **Building trust** (Gartner recognition, customer testimonials)
3. **Enabling self-service evaluation** (Faddom free tier, demo videos)
4. **Industry-specific messaging** (Faddom's vertical pages)

The ROI calculator represents the highest-impact improvement opportunity, followed by customer testimonials and a product demo video. These three items alone would significantly close the competitive gap.
